# Discovery

> "Find the real problem."

## What Happens Here

Talk to users and stakeholders to understand the real problem vs stated problem:
- Who are the users?
- What's working / what's broken?
- Competitive analysis

## Output

**Project Brief** — A clear document capturing the actual problem.
