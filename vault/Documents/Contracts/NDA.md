# Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

> Use this when client shares sensitive business information.

## Parties

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Disclosing Party | [Client Name / Company] |
| Receiving Party | Owen Digitals |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Purpose

The Disclosing Party wishes to share confidential information with Owen Digitals for the purpose of [[Project Name]] project discussions and work.

## Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to:
- Business strategies and plans
- Financial information
- Customer lists and data
- Trade secrets
- Product designs and specifications
- Marketing plans
- Any information marked "confidential"

## Obligations of Receiving Party

Owen Digitals agrees to:
1. Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
2. Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent
3. Use Confidential Information only for the stated purpose
4. Protect Confidential Information with at least same care as own confidential information
5. Limit access to employees/contractors who need to know for the stated purpose

## Exclusions

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Receiving Party
2. Was already known to Receiving Party before disclosure
3. Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
4. Is required to be disclosed by law or court order

## Term

This NDA remains in effect for **2 years** from the date signed.

## Return of Information

Upon request or project completion, Owen Digitals will return or destroy all Confidential Information received.

## Breach

Breach of this NDA may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party. Owen Digitals acknowledges that monetary damages may be inadequate, and the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief.
