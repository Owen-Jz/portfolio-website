### Client Ownership
Upon **full payment**, all final deliverables become the exclusive property of the client.

### Owen Digitals Ownership
- Draft files, working files, and source files remain property of Owen Digitals
- Owen Digitals retains the right to display completed work in portfolio
- Owen Digitals may use project for promotional purposes with client permission

## 6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information shared during the project.

### By Client
- Cancellations must be submitted in writing
- 70% upfront payment is non-refundable once project begins
- Client responsible for payment of all work completed to date of cancellation

### By Owen Digitals
- Owen Digitals may cancel with 14 days written notice
- Full refund of any unspent 70% payment if no work has begun
